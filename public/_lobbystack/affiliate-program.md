# Okjobs Affiliate Program

Refer paying customers to hosted Okjobs plans and earn commission.

## Program terms

| Term | Okjobs |
| --- | --- |
| Commission | 20% |
| Duration | First 12 months after attribution |
| Holding period | 30 days |
| Minimum payout | USD $100 |
| Payout method | PayPal |

Referred businesses get 5% off hosted Okjobs plans when they sign up through your link.

## What pays commission

You earn commission on hosted Okjobs plan payments only. Self-hosting without a paid Okjobs subscription does not pay commission.

## How to get started

1. Sign in and open the affiliate page in your dashboard.
2. Add your PayPal email.
3. Share your link with businesses that need better phone coverage and appointment booking.

## Who should apply

Agencies, consultants, creators, and operators who recommend tools to small businesses, home services, clinics, and salons.
