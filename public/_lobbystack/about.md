# About Okjobs

Okjobs exists to help small businesses answer calls and book appointments without giving up control of their phone workflow or customer data.

## Highlights

- Open-source AI receptionist for small businesses
- Built around call answering, booking, transfers, and call summaries
- Designed for managed cloud use and self-hosted implementation support

## Why Okjobs exists

Most small businesses do not lose customers because they do not care. They lose them because the phone rings while the team is already helping someone else.

- Make every important call visible
- Turn routine questions into handled workflows
- Keep humans in control for sensitive or high-value calls

## Why we built it open source

Phone workflows sit on top of customer data, booking rules, and escalation policies. Teams should be able to inspect how those decisions are made instead of trusting a black box.

- Review the MIT-licensed codebase, deployment model, and data boundaries on GitHub
- Start on Okjobs Cloud and move to self-hosting when your team needs more control
- Avoid vendor lock-in for the receptionist layer that sits in front of every caller

## Who Okjobs is for

Okjobs is built for owner-operators and small teams that live on inbound calls: home services, trades, clinics, salons, and local service businesses that cannot afford to miss ready-to-book callers.

- Teams that miss calls while they are on jobs, in appointments, or closed for the day
- Operators who want phone booking without a phone tree or IVR builder
- Businesses that need after-hours coverage without hiring another full-time receptionist

## How support and security work

Okjobs Cloud handles hosting, monitoring, and product updates. Self-hosted customers run the same open-source stack on infrastructure they control. In both cases, you define what the receptionist can say, book, and escalate.

- Configure allowed answers, booking rules, and transfer paths in plain language
- Review call summaries, transcripts, and outcomes in one operator dashboard
- Use public documentation or contact support@lobbystack.com when you need implementation help
