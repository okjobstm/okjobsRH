Scheduling should not depend on catching every call

When someone calls to book, they are ready to make a decision. If your team misses the call, asks them to wait for a callback, or spends the next hour trading times back and forth, that appointment can disappear.

Missed calls become missed appointments

People looking for service often call more than one business. If they cannot book with you quickly, they may book with the next company that answers.

Manual scheduling slows down your team

Every appointment takes time to confirm, reschedule, log, and explain. That work adds up quickly when your team is already handling customers, jobs, and follow-up.

Back-and-forth costs momentum

A caller who wants to book now should not have to wait for someone to check the calendar and call them back later.

Let callers book while they are still on the phone

Okjobs gives your business an AI appointment scheduler that can answer the call, understand what the caller needs, check your rules, offer times, and book the appointment in the same conversation.

  • Answer booking calls during business hours, after hours, and weekends
  • Collect the caller's name, phone number, service need, location, and timing
  • Offer available appointment windows based on your scheduling rules
  • Book the appointment and send confirmation details
  • Notify your team with the caller's details and reason for booking
  • Transfer urgent or unusual calls to a person when needed

Questions about AI appointment scheduling

What is an AI appointment scheduler?

An AI appointment scheduler helps callers book appointments through a conversation. It can answer the call, collect details, check availability, schedule the appointment, and send confirmation details based on your rules.

Can Okjobs schedule appointments from phone calls?

Yes. Okjobs can answer booking calls, ask the required questions, offer available times, book the appointment, and send the caller a confirmation text.

Can it work with my existing business number?

Yes. You can use a dedicated Okjobs number or forward calls from the business number your customers already call.

Can it schedule appointments after hours?

Yes. Okjobs can answer and schedule calls after hours, on weekends, during holidays, or whenever your team is unavailable.

Can I control what questions it asks before booking?

Yes. You can define the questions Okjobs should ask, such as service type, location, urgency, preferred time, budget, or any details your team needs before the appointment.

What happens if a caller needs help before booking?

You can define handoff rules. Okjobs can answer routine questions, take a message, or transfer the caller to your team when the call needs a person.

Can it handle rescheduling?

Yes. Okjobs can help with simple rescheduling when your rules allow it. More complex scheduling changes can be routed to your team.

Will my team know what was booked?

Yes. Okjobs can send appointment details, caller information, call summaries, transcripts, and next steps to your team after the call.

Is this better than using an online booking form?

It depends on how your customers prefer to book. Online forms work when people are willing to fill them out. Okjobs is better for callers who want to talk, ask questions, and book during the same phone call.
