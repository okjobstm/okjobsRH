Businesses lose money when they miss calls.

A homeowner calls a plumber after hours. A salon client wants to book while the front desk is busy. A clinic receives three calls during lunch. A contractor is on a job site and cannot pick up.

Most of those callers do not wait. They call the next business.

That is why AI receptionists are becoming a practical product to recommend. The problem is obvious, the value is clear, and the customer does not need to replace their whole business system to get started.

The Okjobs Affiliate Program gives you a direct way to earn recurring revenue by helping businesses answer more calls, capture more leads, and book more appointments.

What is the Okjobs affiliate program?

The Okjobs Affiliate Program pays you 20% commission for the first 12 months when a customer signs up through your referral. Referred customers also get 5% off when they start an eligible hosted Okjobs plan through your referral link.

You share Okjobs with businesses that need better phone coverage. When they become a paying customer, you earn a recurring commission while they stay subscribed during their first year.

The arrangement is direct:

  • No voice infrastructure to build.
  • No implementation team to hire.
  • No custom AI stack to maintain.

You bring the customer. Okjobs handles the AI receptionist platform.

Why AI receptionist affiliate programs are growing

AI receptionist affiliate programs are attractive because they sit at the intersection of three strong trends:

  1. Businesses want to stop missing calls.
  2. AI phone agents are becoming good enough for real customer conversations.

The best affiliate products solve a painful problem that customers already understand.
