# What's new with Okjobs?

Product updates, integrations, and improvements from the Okjobs team.

## Updates

- [Calls run on GPT-Live, and website chat can book](https://lobbystack.com/changelog/#gpt-live-calls) - Phone and browser calls now use OpenAI's GPT-Live, website chat can find openings and book, and you choose how your receptionist handles appointments.
- [Change your AI receptionist phone number](https://lobbystack.com/changelog/#phone-number-changes) - You can now replace the phone number your AI receptionist uses from the Okjobs settings page.
- [Test call your AI receptionist and invite your team](https://lobbystack.com/changelog/#test-calls-team-invites) - Okjobs now includes dashboard test calls for your AI receptionist and team member invites for your business workspace.
- [Answering rules now take less time to tune](https://lobbystack.com/changelog/#answering-rules) - Review and adjust receptionist behavior as your team changes how it handles real calls.
- [Cleaner call summaries and follow-up notes](https://lobbystack.com/changelog/#call-summaries) - Call outcomes now use clearer summaries and next-step notes for faster review after a busy day.
